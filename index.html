Who we are

Artists first.

Jazz & The City Band is a New York City–based jazz band made up of dedicated, specialized musicians who are devoted to the art of jazz music.

We are artists first — this music is our specialty, our profession, and the language we speak best. We bring to every occasion the same musicianship, spontaneity, and genuine love for the music that we bring to the bandstand.

True New York jazz.

We bring the sound of true New York jazz to every event we play — from elegant weddings and cocktail hours to corporate events and private celebrations.
